## Scenario sources (for the FICTION chapters)
- "AI 2027" (AI Futures Project, 3 Apr 2025; Kokotajlo, Alexander, Larsen, Lifland, Dean): plausible path with a *superhuman coder* → automated AI research → rapid takeoff; US–China race dynamics. Used as *inspiration for structure only*.
- The film's 2026–2035 years are **invented fiction**, not a prediction. Experts disagree widely; many think these outcomes are unlikely.

## Things deliberately NOT claimed
- No date for Christ's return. No claim that AI *is* a Bible-prophesied entity. No claim that any real company or person did the fictional events.
