# Roadmap: two films, one "illusion" style

**Illusion style** = scene changes are never plain cuts. One thing *becomes* the next: a scroll becomes a phone screen, Daniel's statue becomes a server rack, the sun becomes a data-center light, a zoom into a detail opens the next era. (Match-cuts, iris reveals, infinite-zoom, shape morphs.)

Rule for both films: every claim is labelled **FACT**, **PROPHECY (interpretations differ)** or **FICTION**. No date is ever given for the Return of Christ (Matthew 24:36).

Model credit on the AI film: **Claude Sonnet 5.5 · Effort: Ultracode**.

Workflow ("Continue next step"): finish the next unchecked stage → preview-check frames → render → deliver → tick here → commit + push.
