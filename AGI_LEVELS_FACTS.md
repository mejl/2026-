# AGI LEVELS FILM: facts, judgments, predictions (researched 2 Oct 2026)

## OpenAI's 5 levels (A: Bloomberg, 11 Jul 2024; confirmed by an OpenAI spokesperson; shared at an all-hands)
1 Chatbots (conversational language) · 2 Reasoners (human-level problem solving, "PhD-level without tools") · 3 Agents (take actions for "several days" for a user) · 4 Innovators (help develop new inventions) · 5 Organizations (run an entire company). OpenAI said in Jul 2024 it was "close to level 2". It is OpenAI's own scale, not a scientific standard. Beyond level 5 = ASI (not part of OpenAI's list).

## Where the Gemini prompt was wrong or missing (fixed in the film)
1. Level 1 "starts Q1 2022": no public chatbot existed until Nov 2022 → Jan–Sep 2022 is shown as "the road to level 1".
2. "Claude Opus 5.5 Ultracode" is not a model ("Ultracode" is an effort setting).
3. "Gemini 4 Agent systems" and "Grok 5 multi-agent" in Q3–Q4 2026: neither is released (Gemini 4 reported for late 2026; Grok 5 repeatedly delayed). Shown as EXPECTED, not as facts.
4. Google's first reasoning model was Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking (Dec 2024), not 2025.
5. Level 3 was assigned to all four in the same quarter; evidence says Anthropic first (Feb 2026), OpenAI Apr 2026, Google and xAI Sep 2026.
6. Prompt had no per-lab exact predicted quarters/models for levels 4, 5, ASI → added (table above).
7. ASI was one "2030+" block; you asked for ASI year by year → 2030, 2031, 2032, every quarter.
8. You asked for months instead of "Q1…Q4" → year top-right plus "Jan–Mar" style months everywhere.
9. OpenAI's own definitions (level 3 = "several days") were softened to "hours/days"; nobody has shown days yet.

## Income and society estimates (added 2 Oct 2026; MY ESTIMATES, one person full time, USD, after costs, before tax)

|---|---|---|
| 1 Chatbots | $0 – $1,500 | AI freelancing beginners report ~$500–$1,000/month in the first months |

| 3 Agents | $4,000 – $30,000 | solo-founder micro-SaaS reports $5,000–$50,000 per month in REVENUE with no employees (revenue, not net) |
| 4 Innovators | $15,000 – $150,000 | prediction; only for owners of a good idea |
| 5 Organizations | $30,000 – $500,000+ | prediction; a few solo firms already report $300,000–$500,000/month revenue |
| ASI | no estimate | money itself may stop being the measure |
Society: entry-level hiring declines are debated (Stanford Digital Economy Lab, Nov 2025: ~16% relative employment decline for ages 22–25 in the most AI-exposed jobs; NY Fed Q4 2025: recent-grad unemployment 5.6%; a Federal Reserve study of 1M+ firms found no link to job postings).
